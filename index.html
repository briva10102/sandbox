Automation

What we can build for you.

Four systems are in development and open to pilot projects. Each one is set up around your own documents, tools and workflow.

  • Knowledge Assistant

    Staff ask a question and get an answer from the company's own documents, limited to what each person is allowed to see.

  • Email Automation

    Sorts incoming mail, drafts replies and routes the messages that need a person.

  • Customer Support Assistant

    Answers common customer questions at any hour and hands over to your team when it should.

  • Website Chatbot

    Welcomes visitors, answers questions about your business and collects enquiries.

More workflows will be added as we learn what businesses need. Tell us yours.
